# 🏃 HATUA — The Full App Design

**"Kila hatua ni ushindi mdogo"** — *Every step is a small victory.*

---

## § 01 — Market Analysis

Analysis of the Top 5 global apps with weaknesses and futuristic vision:

1.  **Strava**:
    *   **Weakness**: Overly competitive; can be intimidating for casual walkers.
    *   **Vision**: Social fitness as a lifestyle, not just a competition.
2.  **Nike Run Club**:
    *   **Weakness**: Limited focus on walking and low-intensity activities.
    *   **Vision**: Guided experiences and storytelling during activities.
3.  **MapMyWalk**:
    *   **Weakness**: Clunky UI and heavy reliance on ads in the free version.
    *   **Vision**: Detailed route tracking and data-driven insights.
4.  **Walkmeter**:
    *   **Weakness**: Technical interface that feels outdated.
    *   **Vision**: High-precision data logging and advanced analytics.
5.  **Pacer**:
    *   **Weakness**: Generic community features.
    *   **Vision**: Gamification and simple daily goal tracking.

## § 02 — App Screen Mockups

The interactive prototype features three primary screens within a phone frame:

*   **🔢 Odometer Screen**: Displays distance in metres and kilometres, supports indoor/outdoor modes, and tracks real-time points.
*   **🗺 Live OSM Map**: Integrated OpenStreetMap with an AI route suggestion overlay.
*   **🏆 Leaderboard**: Displays rank tracking and avatar identities (e.g., Simba, Chui).

---

## § 03 — 12 Core Features

1.  **Dual-Mode Odometer**: Seamlessly switch between indoor (pedometer-based) and outdoor (GPS-based) tracking.
2.  **OpenStreetMap (OSM) Integration**: High-quality mapping for route visualization.
3.  **Multi-Constellation GPS**: Support for GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo for ±2m accuracy.
4.  **AI Route Suggestion**: Generates safe and scenic walking routes based on user preferences.
5.  **Safety Mode**: "Walk with me" features and emergency contact alerts.
6.  **M-Pesa Integration**: Direct integration for subscriptions and point redemptions.
7.  **Offline Maps**: Downloadable map regions for areas with poor connectivity.
8.  **Voice Guidance**: Audio cues for distance, pace, and milestones.
9.  **Social Challenges**: Join local or global walking challenges.
10. **Hydration & Health Sync**: Syncs with Apple Health / Google Fit.
11. **Weather Overlay**: Real-time weather updates on the map.
12. **Milestone Badges**: Digital trophies for consistent activity.

## § 06 — Hatua Points Economy

Earn points for:
*   Steps (1 pt per 100 steps)
*   Streaks (Bonus for 7+ days)
*   Challenges (One-off events)

Redeem for:
*   **M-Pesa Cash / Airtime**
*   **Gym Passes / Nike Vouchers**
*   **Marathon Entries**
*   **Environmental Impact**: Plant a tree in Kenya 🌳

## § 08-09 — Tech Stack & Security

*   **Frontend**: React Native
*   **Mapping**: OSM / MapLibre
*   **Backend**: Node.js / Express
*   **Database**: PostGIS / PostgreSQL
*   **Payments**: M-Pesa Daraja API
*   **Security**: AES-256 encryption, Kenya DPA (Data Protection Act) compliant.

## § 10 — 4-Phase Roadmap

1.  **Phase 1 (MVP)**: Core tracking, basic avatars, M-Pesa integration (Kenya focus).
2.  **Phase 2 (Social)**: Leaderboards, social challenges, and AI route suggestions.
3.  **Phase 3 (Ecosystem)**: Partner rewards (Nike, Gyms) and tree planting integration.
4.  **Phase 4 (Regional)**: Expansion to Tanzania, Uganda, and Rwanda.
